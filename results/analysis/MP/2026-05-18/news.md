The market is pricing in a successful operational ramp: record NdPr production, an EPS beat that flipped MP to profitability, and visibility on first magnet sales in H2 2026. The DoD price floor at $110/kg and Apple offtake have de-risked the downside, which is why analysts (Morgan Stanley, Wedbush) keep nudging targets higher. The current week is shaping up to a -10% candle, pushing MP into a pullback zone.
