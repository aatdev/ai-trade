## Net Sentiment: NEGATIVE (short-term, with contrarian setup brewing)

The two-week tape is dominated by post-earnings disappointment, a cascade of analyst PT cuts, and the upheld $495M Similac verdict that re-anchors infant-formula litigation risk. Layered on top: Trump's 100% pharma tariff order (live July 31 for large caps) and an active Section 232 medical-device probe widen the macro overhang precisely where Abbott's import-exposed lines sit. The market is currently discounting the bull case (Exact Sciences synergies, MedDevices +13.2%, dividend support, Ultreon AI clearance) and instead is fading on diagnostics weakness (-18.2%), nutrition (-6%), legal liability, and tariff math. RSI ~26 and a 52-week-low print into May 8 suggest washout territory — sentiment is one-sided bearish, which historically precedes mean-reversion bounces when there's a positive headline catalyst.

## Key Catalysts & Risks (Next 1–4 Weeks)

**Risks (Bearish):**
- Additional NEC/Similac verdicts or class-cert rulings widen estimated liability beyond the $495M anchor.
- New disclosures or expanded recall scope on FreeStyle Libre 2/3 sensors — securities-fraud claims could attract more PT cuts.
- Trump pharma tariff implementation details (large-co. effective date Jul 31, 2026) — any preview headlines amplify cost-pass-through fears for ABT's pharma/diagnostics imports.
- Section 232 medical-device probe could expand tariff regime to ABT's largest segment.
- Sector rotation away from defensive healthcare if rates stay higher-for-longer post-FOMC.
- Further sell-side capitulation (e.g., a major firm moving to Hold) — a Goldman Conviction removal already happened.

**Catalysts (Bullish):**
- Technical: RSI 26 + 52-week low into May 8 — any positive headline (settlement, FDA clearance, Exact Sciences integration win) could trigger violent short-cover bounce.
- New FDA clearances on the MedDevices pipeline (Aveir, structural-heart, Ultreon expansions).
- Exact Sciences synergy update / Cologuard volumes ahead of plan — first integrated data likely on Q2 print.
- Tariff carve-out or "approved onshoring plan" reducing rate from 100% → 20% (regulatory off-ramp exists).
- Dividend reinvestment / Dividend Aristocrat passive flows at sub-$85 levels.
- Insider buying or a buyback expansion announcement.

**Trade view:** Tape is wash-out / oversold with bearish news flow saturating — high-conviction shorts likely covered; favor counter-trend bounce setups toward $90–95, but require a positive headline trigger before sizing — no bottom yet without confirmation; keep stops tight below $81.97 (52-week low) where the next leg down opens.
