## Net Sentiment: MIXED (short-term)

The past two weeks were dominated by the May 14–15 Trump–Xi summit, which delivered headline agreements but no tariff resolution, triggering a "sell-the-news" reaction that pushed KWEB toward its 52-week low (~$26.40). Earnings from Alibaba showed strong AI/cloud momentum (+38% cloud) but collapsing profitability (-62% net income) as the sector front-loads AI capex — a structural positive-but-painful narrative. PBOC easing and steady fund inflows are supportive, but ADR-delisting noise and a fresh China-Russia antitrust pact keep a geopolitical lid on sentiment. Net: constructive macro/AI backdrop offset by trade disappointment and regulatory overhang.

## Key Catalysts & Risks (Next 1-4 Weeks)

**Risks (Bearish):**
- Tariff truce remains vague; Section 301 investigations set to conclude this summer could reintroduce volatility if rates aren't lowered.
- ADR delisting headlines (Bessent "all options on the table") and the China-Russia antitrust alliance keep geopolitical risk elevated.
- Sector-wide AI capex is crushing near-term margins (Alibaba FCF outflow, Tencent earnings growth slowing to low-teens) amid soft Chinese consumption.
- KWEB sitting at 52-week lows with recent 5-day outflows (-$638M) signals weak near-term technical demand.

**Catalysts (Bullish):**
- PBOC easing cycle (rate/RRR cuts, 10-measure stimulus) plus pledged flexible policy for 2026 and consumption subsidies under the 15th Five-Year Plan.
- AI monetization theme: Alibaba cloud +38%, AI revenue triple-digit growth 11 quarters running — a re-rating driver if profitability inflects.
- Improving technicals: MACD positive since May 5, momentum >0 since May 6; 1M/3M net inflows positive.
- Baidu AGM June 5; any further summit follow-through (tariff repeal on ~$30B of goods discussed) could spark a relief rally.

**Trade view:** Range-bound to constructive near 52-week-low support (~$26.20); a tariff-relief or stimulus catalyst could fuel a sharp mean-reversion bounce, but keep stops tight given delisting/geopolitical tail risk.
