## Net Sentiment: MIXED (short-term)

The fundamental Q1 print was a blowout (massive EPS beat, debt cleaned up, FCF positive) and analyst price targets are clustering between $190 and $253 — well above the current price. However, the market spent the last week unwinding the post-earnings rally on a combination of profit-taking, two analyst downgrades to Neutral, and ongoing fears that lithium supply growth will outpace demand and cap pricing power. The setup is classic "good news priced in, watch the lithium tape" — improvement in Chinese lithium carbonate prices (CNY +50% YTD) is the key tailwind that has not yet reasserted itself in the stock.

## Key Catalysts & Risks (Next 1–4 Weeks)

**Risks (Bearish):**
- Lithium spot price reversal — current Chinese rally could fade if new Chilean / Australian supply hits faster than expected
- Further analyst downgrades after Baird/Rothschild moves (momentum effect)
- Macro risk: tariff/EV demand headlines from US-China trade flows
- EV/EBITDA still elevated at ~81x (vs 10y median 14.6x) — multiple compression risk if EBITDA growth slows

**Catalysts (Bullish):**
- Continued strength in Chinese lithium carbonate (CNY 175,000/t and rising)
- China EV charging capacity to double by 2027 (180GW), BESS growth 40–60% in 2026
- Q2 2026 guidance + FCF run rate confirmation if lithium pricing holds
- Index/momentum re-entry: many funds chase >+20% YTD names

**Trade view:** Pullback after a +69% 30-week rally into earnings-driven blow-off top ($221). Fundamentals support a buy-the-dip thesis if technical confirmation appears; otherwise, risk of continuation lower to the next major support is real.
