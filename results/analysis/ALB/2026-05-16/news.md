## Net Sentiment: POSITIVE (short-term) shifting to MIXED (medium-term)

The Q1 print was a structural turning point — Albemarle moved from a "cash-burning lithium victim" narrative (2025) to a "lithium cycle winner with deleveraged balance sheet" story. Spot lithium prices have nearly tripled YoY, and ALB realized $17/kg vs the 2025 average of $10/kg. The stock immediately re-rated higher post-earnings (touched $221 the week of 2026-05-04). However, this week (W of 2026-05-11) the stock printed a large bearish reversal candle from $219 down to $180, suggesting profit-taking and concern that H2 2026 may bring a renewed lithium supply glut (some forecasters call for a 20–22% surplus in 2H).

**Risks (Bearish):**
- Lithium spot prices stalling or pulling back from $28k/t — would directly compress Energy Storage realized prices into Q2.

**Trade view:** Strong fundamental tailwind colliding with stretched price action and a weekly bearish reversal — favors a Long-from-pullback bias rather than chasing strength. The most informative levels to watch are the prior swing $175 (must hold) and EMA200D ~$194 (reclaim to resume uptrend).
