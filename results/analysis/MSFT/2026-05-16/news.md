## Net Sentiment: MIXED-LEANING-BULLISH (short-term)

The market is currently re-rating MSFT on **two competing narratives**. The bullish side dominated this week: Ackman's high-profile new stake plus Wedbush/TD Cowen reaffirmation produced a counter-trend +3% rip on May 15 against a red tape. The OpenAI re-deal removes the worst-case "OpenAI walks away" tail risk and gives Azure a first-look moat through 2032. The bearish counter is the $190B capex bomb dropped at Q3 (~25% above consensus) and the soft Q4 revenue guide — Street is openly debating AI capex/ROI math and operating margin compression to ~44%. Regulatory noise (FTC + EU DMA) is real but slow-burn and not the marginal price driver this week. Net: AI/Azure narrative is winning over capex anxiety *for now*, with Ackman's entry acting as a sentiment anchor.
