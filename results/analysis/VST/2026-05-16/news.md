## Net Sentiment: MIXED (short-term bearish despite bullish fundamentals)

Despite a textbook double beat and reaffirmed guidance on May 7, the market is selling the news. The post-earnings drawdown (~8% in the week, ~30% from the late-January peak of ~$200) reflects three concerns: (1) the $6.13B buyback completed and the next leg of capital return is unclear; (2) FERC scrutiny of co-location deals could erode the AI-utility re-rating premium; (3) leverage rose with the Cogentrix deal pending. The Meta nuclear PPA and Fitch IG upgrade are positive but already priced in earlier.

## Key Catalysts & Risks (Next 1–4 Weeks)

**Risks (Bearish):**
- FERC rulings on data-center co-location pricing could de-rate AI utility names; any concrete restrictions = severe headwind
- Cogentrix deal close (H2 2026) raises gross leverage in the interim — further credit/spread widening risk
- Continued momentum unwind from "AI infra" theme could push VST toward the $125–130 zone and EMA200D area
- Moss Landing lawsuit costs not fully quantified

**Catalysts (Bullish):**
- Confirmation of additional hyperscaler PPAs (similar to Meta deal) would re-ignite the AI bull case
- Cogentrix close on time = capacity addition, EBITDA accretion 2027+
- Buyback expansion / new authorization announcement
- FERC ruling favorable to behind-the-meter co-location structures

**Trade view:** News flow points to short-term weakness as the post-earnings selling pressure dominates and regulatory overhang persists. Medium-term setup remains constructive — wait for technical confirmation of a bottom before adding longs.
