Fundamentals are unambiguously strong — Q1 2026 was a blowout beat, guidance reaffirmed, IG-rating upgrade, and the AI/hyperscaler thesis (Meta, AWS, Microsoft) is intact. However, the tape is in a clear "sell-the-news" correction (-8.2% week, -10.4% month, 5+ red days, stochastics deep oversold) driven by PJM price-collar regulatory risk, JPMorgan/Raymond James valuation cuts, and broad IPP de-rating from peak ~37x to ~19x forward P/E.
